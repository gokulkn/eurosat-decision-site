EuroSAT Decision — Benchmark Results

Fine-tuned torchvision ResNet-18 (ImageNet-pretrained, final layer replaced for the 10 EuroSAT classes) on RGB 64×64 Sentinel-2 patches, with a softmax-confidence abstention layer. Results below are a benchmark on EuroSAT only — not field imagery — from a single run with seed 42. All numbers are copied from reports/TEST_RESULTS.md and reports/test_results.json in the companion repository.

Scope and limitations

  • Benchmark on EuroSAT only; no accuracy is claimed on any other imagery.
  • 64×64 Sentinel-2 patches at 10 m resolution — not field imagery.
  • Single run with one seed (42); no confidence interval computed.
  • Split is our own fixed stratified 70/15/15 train/validation/test split (18,900 / 4,050 / 4,050), not a published benchmark split.
  • torch 2.15.0+cpu, torchvision 0.29.2+cpu; CPU training.
